Privacy information

House of Amelia respects the privacy of people who visit this website and enquire about furnished accommodation in Kenya. This page explains the information used when you browse the website or contact us.

Enquiries and WhatsApp

When you complete an accommodation enquiry, the website prepares a message and opens WhatsApp so you can send it to House of Amelia. The website does not submit that enquiry form to its own server. The message may contain the details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, organisation, travel dates, length of stay, number of occupants and accommodation requirements. WhatsApp processes messages under its own privacy terms.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand general website usage and measure accommodation enquiry activity. The measurement ID used is G-24LLME0GBN. Analytics is not loaded until you choose “Accept analytics”. If you reject optional analytics, the website does not load the Google Analytics tag. Your choice is stored in this browser so it can be remembered on a later visit.

Analytics events may include the page visited, the selected residence, stay duration, enquiry type and campaign source. Names, email addresses, telephone numbers and free-text enquiry notes are not sent as Google Analytics event parameters. Google may process analytics information under its own terms and policies.

Changing your choice

You can change your optional analytics choice at any time using the “Privacy choices” control displayed on the website after you have made a selection. Choosing to reject analytics does not prevent you from viewing residences or contacting House of Amelia through WhatsApp.

Contact

For questions about an accommodation enquiry or this privacy information, contact House of Amelia through WhatsApp or call +254 112 415 949.
